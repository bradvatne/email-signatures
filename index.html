Clubtech email signatures

Source for the signature Clubtech addresses send from, and the public host for the images it loads.

Get your signature

Ops will send you a direct link to your own signatures/<your-name>.html page. Open it, select the whole signature, copy it, and paste it into your mail client's signature box — the full per-client steps are in the install guide.

Add someone new

  1. Drop a square 100 × 100 PNG into images/.
  2. Copy template.html and fill in the five placeholders.
  3. Push, wait for Pages, then install.

Full instructions: README.

Image base URL

https://clubtechglobal.github.io/email-signatures/images/

Signatures already installed keep requesting these exact URLs, so image files here are never renamed or deleted — new photo means a new filename.
